About Me

Gena Hunsaker

EMDR and IFS, trauma-informed therapist. I help individuals understand the human experience, their human experience, in a powerful holistic way. I believe that healing is experienced by understanding ourselves, beyond the identities, expectations, and messages we have assumed, often unconsciously, that have shaped how we experience ourselves and our world. I help individuals relieve suffering and find new ways to experience life and have a more fulfilled human experience.

What is Gena Hunsaker Counseling?

At Gena Hunsaker Counseling, I use EMDR and IFS therapy, mindfulness, and integrative approaches to provide a welcoming and safe environment. My process is always collaborative and respectful, focused on helping clients navigate life’s challenges and develop new skills for coping. I’m passionate about building a positive, therapeutic relationship to support each individual’s journey toward growth and change.
